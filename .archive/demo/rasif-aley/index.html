Our story · قصتنا

The sidewalk of Aley,
from first light to last call.

07:00 — SUNRISE

Morning on the rasif

“Rasif” means sidewalk — and ours wakes up early. Espresso pulled fresh, zaatar croissants out of the oven, and manakich made the way the mountain likes them. Grab it at the drive-through or stay a while.

  • Specialty espresso & matcha
  • Manakich & fresh croissants
  • Breakfast on the go
AFTER DARK

Evenings under the pines

When Aley cools down, the rasif lights up. Hand-shaken cocktails, Lebanese beer and wine, and pizzas from the kitchen — a proper mountain-town night out, minutes from the main road.

  • Signature cocktails & mocktails
  • Local wine, beer & arak-hour vibes
  • Wood-fired pizza till late
NEXT DOOR

Part of the Abo Tarek family

Rasif Aley lives right beside Supermarket Abo Tarek — Aley’s 4.5★ neighborhood market. Do your shopping, then treat yourself on the sidewalk. One stop, twice the reason to visit.
