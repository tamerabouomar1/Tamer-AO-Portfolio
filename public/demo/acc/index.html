Our Legacy

A Lebanese story that reshaped a region's skyline.

Founded in Beirut in 1967 by the Merehbi and Mikati families, ACC grew from a local builder into one of the largest contracting organisations in the Gulf and the wider Middle East — a family-held company that never lost its founding discipline.

Today, from Beirut to Abu Dhabi to Jeddah and across Africa, ACC self-delivers the developments others consider impossible: taller, faster, and to an exacting standard.

  • Self-perform capability across core trades
  • Disciplined risk management on every build
  • Proven delivery of super-tall & mega-projects
