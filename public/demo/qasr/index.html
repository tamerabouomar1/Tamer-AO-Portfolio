Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if a date is available?

On WhatsApp at +961 81 950 370. We reply as soon as possible.

Can we visit before booking?

Yes, by appointment. A visit covers the space, the layout and every question in person.

Can the Qasr host two events on the same day?

Yes. One date can hold a daytime event and an evening event, so the time of the event matters as much as the date.

Is there an indoor option?

Yes. The Qasr has an exclusive indoor salon for more private celebrations. A premium option, priced on request.

How much does an event cost?

Pricing depends on the date, the guest count and what is included. With those three, the Qasr gives a clear price on WhatsApp.
