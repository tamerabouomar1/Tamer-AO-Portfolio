Our story

From Textile Waste to Dignified Work.

FabricAID Uniforms is part of FabricAID, the social enterprise founded in 2017 by Omar Itani to build a fairer value chain for the apparel industry. Today FabricAID is the largest second-hand clothing collector in the MENA region.

Our uniforms line grew out of that mission. The same facility and skilled tailors, many trained in our own school, now produce uniforms for organisations that want quality and purpose in one order. Discover the wider mission at fabricaid.me.
