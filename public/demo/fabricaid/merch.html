The idea

Scrap Is Our Raw Material.

After sorting, roughly 16% of the clothing we collect is torn shirts, worn jeans and fabric that can't be resold in a dignified way. Instead of sending it to landfill, we make it the starting point.

We have collected over 100 tonnes of scrap fabric since we began, cleaning, cutting and refashioning it into a wide range of branded merchandise for corporations. It is the same waste, given a second, more useful life.
