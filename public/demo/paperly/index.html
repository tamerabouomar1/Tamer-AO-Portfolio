Questions

Does it really print without ink?

Yes. It prints with heat onto thermal paper, so there are no cartridges, toner or ribbons to buy. Paper is the only thing you ever reload.

What paper does it use?

216 mm (8.5 inch) thermal paper, in A4 or US Letter size, on a roll or folded. Regular office paper will not print. Every printer comes with a free starter roll.

How long do prints last?

Thermal prints are made for everyday use: notes, homework, forms, contracts, shipping labels. They can fade over the years with heat and sunlight, so for papers you must keep long term, store them somewhere cool or keep a scan.

What can I print from?

iPhone and Android over Bluetooth with the free app, and Windows or Mac over the USB cable.

Does it print in colour?

No. It prints in crisp black at 300 dpi, which suits documents, text and line drawings.

How does the paper subscription work?

Choose Subscribe & save and a paper pack ships every 8 weeks at the member price. Skip, change or cancel anytime from your account.

What if it is not for me?

Send it back within 30 days for a full refund. It is also covered by a 1-year warranty.
